Sayri Mobile v0.1.1

The AI Operating System Copilot on Android

System AI Assistant GPL-3.0

Sayri Mobile is the official companion assistant for Pulsar OS, connecting your phone directly to your desktop or running independently:

  • System Orchestration: Dynamic discovery of all installed apps and deep App Functions/Intents integration.
  • Spatial Perception & OCR: Circle to Search and screen layout analysis with exact pixel coordinates.
  • Voice Dictation: Word-by-word streaming offline recognition powered by Dicio.
  • Dual Device Control: Seamlessly trigger actions on your smartphone or send commands to Pulsar OS on PC.

How to add manually:

  1. Open your F-Droid client (F-Droid, Droid-ify, or Neo Store).
  2. Go to Settings → Repositories → Add Repository (+).
  3. Paste the repository URL: https://inledgroup.github.io/fdroid-repo/repo
  4. Tap Add and refresh repositories. Sayri Mobile will now appear ready to install!
